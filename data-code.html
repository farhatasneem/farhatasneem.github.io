Data and codes

Replication packages for my papers, and datasets built along the way that are useful on their own. Everything runs end to end from raw inputs, and each repository has a README describing the data sources, the order the scripts run in, and what the outputs are. Replace the # links below with your repository URLs once they are public.

Replication packages

  • coastal-salinity-replication

    Replication package for Coastal Salinity and Economic Development: panel construction, the salinity index, and the estimation and figures.

    StataPython

    Repository

  • worldpop-bbs-validation

    Validates WorldPop gridded population estimates against the Bangladesh Bureau of Statistics 2022 census at district level, and documents systematic overestimation in the Chittagong Hill Tracts districts. Includes a complete Stata replication of the analysis.

    StataPythonGeoPandas

    Repository

Datasets

  • bangladesh-ntl-panel

    A union-year night-time lights panel for Bangladesh, built from VIIRS monthly composites with zonal statistics over union boundaries. A reusable base layer for any project needing night-time luminosity at union level.

    PythonrasteriorasterstatsGeoPandas

    Repository

  • bbs-population-pyramids

    Extraction pipeline that turns the population pyramids in the 2011 and 2022 BBS district reports into tidy age-sex tables.

    Pythonpdfplumberpdftotext

    Repository

  • bd-industrial-zones

    A geospatial dataset of Bangladesh’s industrial zones — export processing zones, special economic zones and BSCIC estates — with locations matched to administrative boundaries.
