Welcome

I’m an Economics graduate and currently a Research Analyst at the Bangladesh Institute of Development Studies (BIDS), where I work on questions at the intersection of development and environmental economics.

I’m interested in how economic life takes shape, and how it changes when the world around it does. Much of my current work looks at the relationship between environmental change and economic activity in Bangladesh, particularly in places where shifting coastlines, changing land conditions, and livelihoods meet. I enjoy the process of piecing together different kinds of evidence, from satellite imagery and spatial data to census records and household surveys, to make sense of patterns that aren’t always obvious from any one source. Along the way, I’ve developed a particular fondness for making data tell a story, and for questioning the stories it seems to tell too easily.

Outside research, music takes up a fair bit of my time. My tastes wander between the Beatles, ABBA, Boney M., and Indian classical music, with plenty of detours in between. I also bake a rather good banana bread, enjoy a spirited board or card game, and rarely turn down an opportunity for a good conversation.
