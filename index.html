Welcome

I am a Research Analyst at the Bangladesh Institute of Development Studies (BIDS), where I work with the Research Director on project design, survey instruments and grant applications, and on data collection and analysis for ongoing studies.

My research is in development and environmental economics. I am interested in how climate and environmental change shape local economic activity in Bangladesh, and much of my work combines satellite and spatial data — night-time lights, remote-sensed soil salinity, gridded population — with census and household survey microdata.

I hold an M.S.S. (2025) and a B.S.S. (2024) in Economics from the University of Dhaka. Away from economics I have been a student of music at the Benuka Institute of Fine Arts since 2009. [Delete this last sentence if you would rather keep the site strictly professional.]
